🔬 Cell Confluency Analyzer

Upload microscope images to automatically calculate cell confluency

How to use:

  • Upload a cell culture microscope image (phone or camera)
  • Toggle auto-detect or manually define the microscope field by clicking to set the center
  • The app uses Laplacian edge detection to identify cell boundaries
  • Adjust edge threshold to fine-tune cell detection sensitivity
  • Use morphology iterations to fill cell interiors and close gaps
  • Works best with phase contrast or brightfield microscopy images
